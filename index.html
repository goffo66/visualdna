VISUAL DNA - Privacy Notice

Preliminary GitHub Version | Last updated: 12 October 2026
Data Controller: Geoff Foster

What this test is

A 60-second, 50-image exercise to identify preference across 5 photographic genres: Architecture, Nature, Street, Human Interest, and Creative (abstract imagery).

What we collect

  • Your 50 Pass / Like it choices
  • Technical data: IP address, approximate location from IP (city level), device/browser type, date/time
  • We do NOT collect name, email, precise location or account details. No tracking cookies.

Legal basis (UK GDPR)

Security and abuse prevention and research into aggregate visual preference - legitimate interests. All published analysis uses only grouped, anonymised data.

Where your data goes

  • Hosting: Hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA - owned by Microsoft). GitHub may collect IP and technical data in server logs when you load the page. See: docs.github.com
  • Storage: Your choices and our security log are stored via Basin (processor) in the EU.

How long we keep it

  • Our Basin security log including IP: 30 days then deleted
  • After that, your choices are kept in a form that cannot identify you, for research
  • GitHub's own server logs are retained under GitHub's own policy

Your rights

Your IP address is personal data, but we only keep it for 30 days for security, then we delete it. After that, your choices cannot be linked back to you.

Because this test is anonymous - we don't ask for your name or email - we have no way to tell which choices are yours if you contact us later, unless you happen to still have your IP address and the exact time you took the test. This protects your anonymity.

You have the right to object to our processing for research, and the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) - www.ico.org.uk - if you have concerns.

Children

General audience - no explicit or sensitive content. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
